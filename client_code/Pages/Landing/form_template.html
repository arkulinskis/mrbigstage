Questions

What does "confidence" mean here?

Listeners hear confidence as a mix of things, so we measure each one: your pace (words a minute and whether you rush), pauses, hesitation sounds, volume and projection, pitch variety (melody vs monotone), steadiness and timbre (shaky or breathy vs full), how your sentences end (rising like a question or fading), eye contact and gestures, and timing. Each gets a score; the Stage Confidence score blends them, weighted by how much audiences notice them.

Do I need a VR headset?

No. It's built for Meta Quest and other WebXR headsets (open the site in the headset's browser and press Enter VR), but it works on any computer or phone too. On a flat screen, eye contact is measured by where you look with the mouse.

Is my voice recorded or uploaded?

Your microphone is analysed live inside your browser. We save only the numbers (like your pace and how many pauses you made). If you turn on recording, the audio stays in your browser so you can listen back; it is never sent to us.

How accurate is it?

It's a coach, not a lab. Pace and pauses are close; hesitation sounds and sentence endings are good estimates. The most useful thing is the trend: the same measurements, every time, so you can see yourself improve.

Can I try it without an account?

Yes. Try the sample talk or any drill right now. An account (free) saves your talks, reports and progress.

Who is it for?

Anyone who has to speak: a wedding toast, a job interview, a class presentation, a team update, a conference talk, or just being less scared of putting your hand up.
