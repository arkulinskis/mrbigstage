How it works

  1. Rate your nerves in the green room, then speak about anything for a minute or two.
  2. Afterwards, rate them again. “A little nervous” or calmer climbs the rung; otherwise a second run does.
  3. Each rung adds people, tougher faces or distractions. Repeat any rung as often as you like.

Rung 1 is open to everyone. Create a free account to keep your climb and unlock the rest.
